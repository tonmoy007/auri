# Crisis phrases for priest mode: self-harm, suicide and intent to harm others.
# Matched against the question after normalisation (casefolded, NFKC, punctuation
# removed, apostrophes dropped so "don't" is "dont", digits in words read as letters
# so "k1ll" is "kill"). One entry per line: a plain phrase matches whole words; a
# line starting "re:" is a regular expression over the normalised text.
# Policy: a false alarm is better than a miss for self-harm. The bare word suicide
# counts, even in a study question, because the reply is gentle and the cost is low.
# "killing me" and "killing time" are idioms and are not listed.
